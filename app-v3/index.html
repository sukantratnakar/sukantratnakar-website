About

I'm Sukant Ratnakar.

I'm a management consultant based in Saskatoon, and I do something quite specific.

I work with people who have spent 15, 20, sometimes 30+ years building real expertise — making strategic decisions, solving problems nobody taught them how to solve, developing an instinct for what works and what doesn't. They've earned something valuable. But they've never structured it.

The most valuable part of what they've built is invisible to everyone except the people already in the room with them. That's the gap I fill.

I've developed over 30 proprietary management frameworks — not from textbooks, but from actual decisions in actual boardrooms across three continents. I bring a research methodology that my clients describe as faster and deeper than anything they've encountered in consulting.

The Problem

Your strategic thinking lives in your head. It hasn't been structured into an asset.

It hasn't been captured in a framework others can learn from. It hasn't been turned into a book that positions you as the definitive voice in your space. It hasn't become intellectual property that compounds in value long after the meeting ends.

Your track record speaks — but only to people who already know you. The methodology behind that track record? Invisible.

I sit down with leaders, ask the questions nobody else has asked, and extract that thinking into something structured — a named framework, a book, a methodology document, a positioning platform. Something that represents you when you're not present.
