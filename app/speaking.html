The 45-minute talk

The signature talk: Change Anesthesiology

For twenty years, roughly seven in ten change initiatives have fallen short — an estimated $2.3 trillion a year. The frameworks have multiplied; the number has not moved.

My argument is that we have been treating the wrong condition. Resistance is not the obstacle — it is the signal. It tells you something true about the conditions upstream: drained energy, narrow perspective, minds already full before your project arrived. The real work happens before the initiative begins.

Hence the name — in 1846, anaesthesia did not make surgery better, it made surgery survivable.

What your people take away

  • A different first question: not who is resisting? but what conditions made resistance sensible?
  • A four-stage model they can apply to a change already underway, or one still on the drawing board.
  • A practical read on why past initiatives stalled, before the next one launches.
